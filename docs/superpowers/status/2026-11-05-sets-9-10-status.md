The ambience has one density per scene, so a rarer bell needs a per-source density through `AmbienceScene`, `AmbienceAU` and the C API. That is a restructure for one source. Deferred to the next ambience run. Until then the garden's bell strikes as often as the tea house's (every 2–5 minutes).

## Ear-and-eye checklist for Michael

**Sound**
1. **Koto press (oshi):** pluck a note and push the wheel up. The string should glide up a whole tone and back with no new pluck. The wheel's lower half bends the string down too; say if you'd rather it only raised.
2. **Koto yuri:** lean into a long note (aftertouch). A slow, uneven vibrato should start a moment after the pluck. With a poly-pressure keyboard, a controller reset (CC 121) should now stop it.
3. **Shakuhachi meri and muraiki:** lean into a long note; it should sink up to a semitone and a half, darken, and rise back as you ease off. Strike hard and the note should burst with breath.
4. **The bell in your key:**
   - Play for a while in one key. The bell's next strike (every few minutes, fuller from dusk in the tea house) should ring that key's keynote, low.
   - A strike that is already ringing should never change pitch.
   - Until a key is heard, the bell rings E.
   - In the garden it strikes as often as in the tea house (deferred: it should be rarer).

**Sky and weather**
5. **Rime:** pick Rime frost. On a foggy morning the edges should feather white over 10–20 minutes (Advanced → Rime speed hurries it) and melt in sun once the fog goes. Check the bedroom now sounds a little more hushed in rime.
6. **Iridescence:** pick Light cloud with the sun 5–50° up. Thin cloud near the sun should show soft pinks and greens along its edges; there should be no rainbow bands and no brightening beyond the cloud's own light.

**The worlds and cards**
7. **The garden:** the water-knocker you see and the one you hear keep separate times. Say whether they should clack together.
8. **Smooth:** both worlds on Smooth on the Intel MacBook Pro should stay fluid.
9. **The two place cards:**
   - The tea house card shows 13 faint strings along the lower edge; the garden card shows 5 bamboo rings along the top.
   - Both breathe slowly, show the set's name and mood, and have no Listen button.
